    How to play
    • Tap the spinner for 1–6, then move your own piece: tap each space in turn and count them off, just like at a table. space works for both. Tap a wrong square and the board nudges you back on track.
    • Pieces wait off the board to begin with, so your first count of one brings you onto space 1.
    • Everyone spins once at the start to see who goes first — highest wins, and a tie spins again. Turn it off in the setup if you'd rather just play.
    • Click anyone's colour dot — in the setup or beside their name mid-game — to change it.
    • 🍬 Candy canes carry you up. Land on one and it starts glowing — tap the cane itself and your piece climbs it.
    • 🌀 Sugar slides drop you back down. Tap the slide and your piece rides the curve to the bottom.
    • ⭐ Star space — spin again right away.
    • 🍮 Sticky space — you lose your next turn.
    • You must land on 64 exactly. Overshoot and you bounce back.

# Life Paths (board game)

This is a fully self-contained game (one file, no
dependencies), inspired by Life-style board games, for 2–4 players on one
shared screen. Open it in a browser and share it over Zoom.

Players tap the spinner, then move their own piece from Childhood to
Grown-Up Life. Their own space is marked 📍 with their name; they either tap
the "👣 Step here" space just ahead once per step (counting as they go), or
count in their head and tap the space they'll land on. Each space moved makes
a wooden click. The spaces are built
to get people talking rather than just clicking:

- **💬 What would you do?** A real social situation (a new kid alone at lunch,
  keeping a secret, a coworker taking credit) with three choices. The heart and
  coin effects only show *after* a choice, followed by a "Talk about it"
  question. When another player answers, tap their name and both get +1 ❤️.
- **🎤 Share Time:** answer a question out loud, then another player asks a
  follow-up question (starter phrases are on screen). Both get +1 ❤️.
- **🧘 Show a Coping Skill:** an "Imagine..." moment (lost a game, plans
  canceled), then the player picks one of three calm-down strategies and
  actually does it for the group: Box Breathing, Smell the Flower / Blow Out
  the Candle, 5-4-3-2-1 Senses, Squeeze and Let Go, Positive Self-Talk, Turtle
  Shell, and more (12 in all). The breathing ones have a guided circle with a
  countdown to follow. Everyone can join in for +1 ❤️.
- **⚡ Oops!** A setback, a choice about how to cope with it, and a "Show us!"
  prompt to demonstrate a calm-down strategy.
- **🤝 Help a Friend**, **🎁 Life Events** (some with a quick act-it-out
  prompt), and **💰 Payday**.
- **🛑 Stop spaces:** go to college or start working, pick a career, and pick
  a home.
- At the **🎉 Life Party**, Life Points = coins + 10 per ❤️ + home value.
  Everyone gets an award and a "life story" of the choices they made, followed
  by group reflection questions.

For the group leader: 🔄 swaps a card that doesn't suit your group,
🔊 Read / 🗣️ Auto-read reads cards aloud (tick "Share sound" in Zoom),
🏁 End Game jumps to the Life Party when time runs short, and a Quicker game
option uses a 1–10 spinner. Everything is click-only for Zoom Remote Control,
and the board fits a 1600×900 or larger window without scrolling.

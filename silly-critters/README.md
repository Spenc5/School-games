# Silly Critters

A spin-and-build game for two players aged 5–6, made for the same Zoom
screen-share + Remote Control setup. It's one
self-contained file you open in a browser. It works offline, and all the
art is original inline SVG.

- **Part chart** (always on screen): 1 = body, 2 = head, 3 = eyes,
  4 = legs, 5 = nose, 6 = hat, 7 = arms, 8 = star. Each tile has the same
  color as its wedge on the spinner. A critter needs a body first. Legs
  and arms need a body. Eyes, nose and hat need a head. Landing on the
  **star** lets you pick any part you need. On the chart, a check means the current
  player already has that part and a lock means they can't add it yet.
- **Name badges:** after picking Race or Team, Player 1 and then Player 2
  each pick a silly name badge (Captain Wiggles, Noodle Nose, Fuzzy
  Muffin and more), each with its own picture and color. A badge one
  player took is greyed out for the other. A player can also type their
  own name (up to 16 letters) and tap OK, which gives them a smiley badge
  in their player color. **No name** keeps "Player 1/2". The names appear on each panel, in the coach line and on the Go
  button. Play again keeps the same badges.
- A **coach line** under the chart says in plain words what to do next,
  for example "Player 1, tap the spinner! Next you need legs."
- **Tap the spinner** to spin it. It slows down and stops on its own. A
  gold pointer and a pink pointer each point at a part, and the matching
  chart tiles light up gold and pink. The player taps the part they want,
  then picks one of 3 silly versions, each previewed on their own critter.
  Every part has 6 versions, and the 3 offered are chosen at random each
  time, so the choices change from turn to turn.
  A happy chime plays when at least one part fits, and a sad
  "wah-wah-wah-waaah" trombone plays when neither does.
- If neither part fits, a short card says "Neither one fits. You get a
  spare part!" and the player gets a **spare-part cog** (up to 3) without
  having to pick. In Team mode, if neither fits you but one fits your
  teammate, you get the cog and then tap the gift for them. With 3 cogs,
  the player's next turn starts with a card asking whether to use them to
  pick any part (a cog button under the spinner does the same). They
  still spin after.
- When one part fits and the other doesn't, the one that doesn't fit
  looks the same but tapping it does nothing, so a mis-tap never costs a
  turn.
- Twice per game: **Swap** lets the player trade one part type with the
  other critter (the red X skips it). The first Swap comes after about
  4–6 spins and the second 4–6 spins later, once both critters have the
  same kind of part in different styles.
- **The game never switches players on its own.** At the end of a turn, a
  big "Go" button with the next player's name waits for a tap, which leaves time to
  pass Zoom remote control. Result cards also wait for a tap on OK.
- **Race**: the first finished critter wins.
- **Team**: finish both critters before the sleepy owl runs out of
  18 moons (one per turn). The part you don't take is a gift for your
  teammate. Once your own critter is done, you spin and choose which part
  to give.
- On a phone held upright the layout rearranges: the chart sits on top,
  the two critters side by side, and the spinner underneath.
- At the end both critters dance and a big **Play again** button appears.
  The ? button shows the rules in pictures, the 🏠 button goes back to the
  mode picker (tap it twice during a game), and the speaker button mutes
  sound. Space or Enter spins and starts the next turn. Keys 1 and 2 pick
  the gold or pink part.

🎲 School Games

Click-to-play games for sessions. Each one works in any browser. Share the window in Zoom and give Remote Control to whoever's turn it is.

♟Chess 2 players · or vs. computer Click a piece, then click where it goes. Legal moves are highlighted. 🍭Sugar Summit 2–4 players Spin, ride the candy canes up, dodge the sugar slides down. First to space 64 wins. 🐛Silly Critters 2 players · ages 5–6 Spin the wheel and build a silly critter one part at a time. 🛤️Life Paths 2–4 players A Life-style board game with "What would you do?" situations, share time, and coping skills. 🎴Matching Game 2 players Flip two cards at a time to find pairs. Most pairs wins.

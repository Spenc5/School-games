    Using this with Zoom

    1. Open this page in your browser and share this window/tab in Zoom (Share Screen → pick the browser window).
    2. When it's a student's turn, use Zoom's Remote Control feature (in the Zoom toolbar during screen share) so they can click on your screen from their own computer.
    3. Players just click a piece, then click a highlighted square to move it — no dragging needed, which keeps it reliable over remote control.
    4. Turn on "Auto-flip board" so the board always faces the player whose turn it is — handy when control passes back and forth on one shared screen.
    5. Use Undo if a student wants to try a move again — great for coaching without restarting the whole game.
    6. Set Opponent to one of the Computer levels to play on your own — useful for trying the board out before a session, or letting a student play a solo game. The computer plays Black.

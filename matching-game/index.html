Using this with Zoom

  1. Open this page and share this window/tab in Zoom (Share Screen → pick the browser window).
  2. Players take turns flipping two cards at a time, trying to find matching pairs.
  3. Use Zoom's Remote Control feature (in the screen-share toolbar) so whoever's turn it is can click cards on your screen from their own computer.
  4. Find a match and you keep your turn and score a point; no match, and the cards flip back and it's the other player's turn.
  5. The player with the most pairs when the board clears wins. Use New Game to shuffle and play again.
